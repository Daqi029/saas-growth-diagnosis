# Contributing / 贡献说明

Issues and pull requests that improve diagnostic accuracy, language quality, scope boundaries, or test coverage are welcome.

欢迎提交能够改善诊断准确性、多语言表达、能力边界或测试覆盖的 Issue 和 Pull Request。

## Privacy and case policy / 客户与隐私规则

- Do not submit client names, URLs, analytics, screenshots, recordings, interview notes, repositories, or other non-public information without the client's explicit written permission.
- Public examples must be fictional, synthetic, thoroughly anonymized, or explicitly authorized for publication.
- Publicly reachable information is not automatically an authorized client case. Do not publish an inferred diagnosis of an identifiable client.
- Remove secrets and personal data before submitting. When uncertain, describe the product category and behavior abstractly.
- Maintainers may remove a contribution that creates a privacy, confidentiality, or attribution risk.

- 未经客户明确书面许可，不得提交客户名称、网址、数据、截图、录屏、访谈、仓库或其他非公开信息。
- 公开案例必须是虚构、合成、彻底匿名化，或者已经明确获得公开授权。
- 信息可以公开访问，不代表可以把可识别客户作为公开诊断案例。
- 提交前删除密钥和个人信息；不确定时只描述抽象的产品类型和行为。

By contributing, you confirm that you have the right to submit the material and agree that it will be distributed under the repository's existing license.
