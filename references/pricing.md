# Pricing and packaging diagnosis

Use this reference when activated users reach pricing or upgrade surfaces but cannot understand, choose, or justify a plan.

## Separate four problems

1. Value problem: users do not want the outcome enough. Pricing-page polish will not fix it.
2. Packaging problem: the paid boundary does not match meaningful jobs, usage, or segments.
3. Comprehension problem: users cannot tell which plan fits or what changes after upgrading.
4. Purchase-risk problem: users understand the offer but fear commitment, security, cancellation, or procurement friction.

## Pricing-page checks

- Are the primary self-serve choices limited enough to compare?
- Is one recommended plan tied to a clear use case rather than decorative emphasis?
- Are plan differences concrete, grouped, and scannable?
- Are quantities explicit instead of “more” or “advanced”?
- Is monthly versus annual billing unambiguous?
- Does annual billing show the actual billing commitment and truthful savings?
- Are Enterprise or custom-sales paths separated when their buying process differs?
- Are currency, tax, invoice, refund, cancellation, and support expectations clear where relevant?
- Is the unit of value aligned with how customers receive value?

The source article recommends three core self-serve tiers and visually highlighting the middle plan. Treat this as a testable pattern, not a universal law. Some products need one plan; others need more because they serve genuinely distinct buying motions.

## Evidence to request

- pricing-page visits → plan selections → checkout starts → purchases;
- selection and paid conversion by plan;
- annual versus monthly selection and retention;
- upgrade and cancellation interviews;
- sales questions about plan fit;
- expansion, downgrade, and overage behavior;
- willingness-to-pay evidence only as supporting input, not a substitute for purchase behavior.

## Candidate experiments

- Repackage tiers around distinct customer jobs or maturity levels.
- Make the recommended plan's “best for” condition explicit.
- Replace vague feature differences with concrete quantities and outcomes.
- Show annual commitment and actual savings without hiding total billing.
- Move Enterprise to a distinct sales path when self-serve comparison is distorted.
- Add reassurance beside the purchase action for the specific observed risk.
